Aftercare After Acne and Peeling Treatments

Questions

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us.

Have you had an acne or peeling treatment?

To help you recover comfortably, here are the general precautions and the precautions for each treatment. Please check the ones that match your treatment.

General Precautions

Every day

Moisturizing and sun protection

Please keep your skin well moisturized and protected from the sun.

Contact us

Temporary reactions you may notice

Inflammation and flaking may temporarily appear after treatment. If it is severe, or if you have any questions, please contact the clinic.

Precautions by Treatment

Please check the section for your treatment.

Oxygen Peel (산소필)
  • See general precautions

Please refer to the general precautions.

Aqua Peel (아쿠아필)
  • Bruise-like marks fade in 3–4 days
  • Do not touch breakout areas

Temporary bruise-like marks may appear and fade after 3–4 days, so there is no need to worry.

If you had sebum buildup or acne, breakouts may occur around those areas. Do not touch or irritate them.

Wecouno (위코우노)
  • Stop retinol and vitamin C for 3 days
  • Moisturizer and recovery cream

Tingling and a cooling sensation are normal reactions.

To keep your skin from drying out after treatment, apply plenty of moisturizer and recovery cream.

For about 3 days, stop using cosmetics containing retinol or vitamin C, as they may irritate the skin.

Aladdin Peeling (알라딘필링)
  • No washing or cosmetics on the day
  • Do not remove or wash off the powdery flakes
  • No saunas or jjimjilbang until flaking is over

Washing and applying cosmetics are not allowed on the day of treatment. It may feel prickly, like thorns, but please moisturize well.

You may feel something powdery on your skin after treatment. Please don't pick it off or wash it away; let it fall off naturally.

Please avoid saunas and jjimjilbang until the flaking has fallen off.

If flaking makes your skin look untidy, press moisturizing cream onto it rather than rubbing (to avoid removing it artificially).

Avoid spraying the shower directly onto your face or body; it is better to wash under running water.

If your body was also treated, start using body wash from 3 days later.

Calming (카밍)
  • Flaking and scabs possible
  • Cold-water washing recommended
  • Contact us if there is oozing

Flaking may occur. Occasionally, if the reaction is strong, scabs may form. In that case, care for the area with moisturizing cream, and washing with cold water is recommended.

If there is any oozing, please contact the clinic.

Black Peel (블랙필)
  • Flaking and breakouts possible
  • Do not touch or irritate

Flaking may occur, and breakouts may appear. Do not touch or irritate the skin.

SEBO
  • Nearby inflammation possible
  • Do not touch or irritate

Inflammation may appear around the treated area. Do not touch or irritate it.

AGNES (아그네스)
  • Do not touch the treated area
  • Avoid heavy drinking, saunas, jjimjilbang, bathhouses, and strenuous exercise

After treatment, take care to keep your hands off the treated area.

It is best to avoid heavy drinking, saunas, jjimjilbang, bathhouses, and strenuous exercise.

Gold PTT
  • Light washing, showering and makeup OK on the day
  • Avoid saunas, alcohol, smoking and strenuous exercise for 1 week
  • Breakouts may appear for 2–3 days

Light washing, showering, and makeup are fine on the day of treatment, but avoid scrubs or anything that irritates the skin.

For 1 week after treatment, avoid hot saunas, jjimjilbang, hot baths, alcohol, smoking, and strenuous exercise that makes you sweat.

Itching, stinging, and redness may occur after treatment, but they go away naturally, so there is no need to worry.

Breakouts may appear for about 2–3 days after treatment and go away naturally, so there is no need to worry. If it bothers you, we can perform extraction—please contact the clinic.

If you have any concerns during aftercare, please don't wait—let us know. The doctor who treated you will check.
