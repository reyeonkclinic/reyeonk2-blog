Aftercare After Pigmentation, Redness and Post-Acne Red Mark Treatments

Questions

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us.

Have you had a pigmentation, redness or post-acne red mark treatment?

To help you recover comfortably, here are the general precautions and the precautions for each treatment. Please check the ones that match your treatment.

General Precautions

Before and after treatment

Sun protection

Reapply sunscreen during the day and wear a hat. Before and after treatment, use a sunscreen that also blocks UVA.

On the day of treatment

Washing your face

On the day of treatment, wash your face gently with lukewarm or cold water rather than warm water.

No-go for 1 week

Take good care of moisturizing

Moisturizing is essential. Please avoid the items below for about 1 week.

  • Sauna
  • Jjimjilbang (Korean sauna)
  • Strenuous exercise

Whitening (Pigmentation)

Please check the section for your treatment.

Laser Toning & Spectra Peel
  • See general precautions

Please follow the general precautions.

IPL
  • Contact us if redness lasts 3+ days or blisters form
  • Do not scrub hard
  • Darkening for about 1 week is normal

If redness lasts for 3 days or more, or blisters form on the treated area, please contact the clinic.

Avoid scrubbing hard when washing your face. Pigmentation may look darker for about 1 week after treatment, but this is a normal reaction.

PicoSure Laser
  • Do not pick at scabs
  • Thorough moisturizing
  • Contact us if blisters form

Scabs may form. Don't pick them off—let them fall off naturally.

Moisturize thoroughly. If blisters form on the treated area, please contact the clinic.

PicoSure Toning
  • See general precautions

Please follow the general precautions.

Mole Removal
  • DuoDERM stays on for 1 week
  • Reapply if it falls off
  • Sunscreen after removal

We apply a healing tape (DuoDERM) over the treated area. If it comes off within 1 week, please apply a new one.

Light washing and makeup should be done with the DuoDERM still on. (Be careful not to let water get under it.)

After removing the DuoDERM at 1 week, apply sunscreen well.

Facial Redness · Post-Acne Red Marks

Please check the section for your treatment.

Synergy
  • Heat sensation possible
  • Moisturize thoroughly after icing
  • Sun protection
  • Avoid saunas and jjimjilbang during the treatment period

You may feel heat after treatment.

Icing at home is fine, but you must moisturize thoroughly after icing.

Please protect the treated area from the sun carefully.

It is best not to go to saunas or jjimjilbang during the treatment period.

If you have any concerns during aftercare, please don't wait—let us know. The doctor who treated you will check.
