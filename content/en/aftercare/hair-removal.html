Hair Removal: Before and After Care Instructions

Questions

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us.

Are you having a hair removal treatment?

Here is how to prepare before treatment and how to care for your skin afterward. Please check both the before and after instructions.

Before Your Hair Removal Treatment

Preparation

Shave cleanly

Please shave the area to be treated cleanly before you come.

Check

If you have wounds or inflammation

If there are wounds or inflammation in the treatment area, it is better to treat them first.

Please avoid

Waxing and other forced hair pulling

Shaving is fine, but please avoid pulling hair out forcibly, such as waxing.

When to visit

If you've had waxing

If you have had waxing, please visit 3–4 weeks afterward.

After Your Hair Removal Treatment

No need to worry

Remaining hairs fall out on their own

Hairs remaining on the skin after treatment naturally fall out within 1–2 days, so there is no need to worry.

Every day

Sun protection and moisturizing

Apply sunscreen carefully whenever you go out, and keep your skin well moisturized.

Medical visit

Please visit us in these cases

Redness or swelling may appear on the treated skin, which usually subsides over time. If it does not go away after some time has passed, or if hives appear, please visit our clinic for an examination.

If you have any concerns during aftercare, please don't wait—let us know. The doctor who treated you will check.
