Aftercare After Skin Boosters, Fillers, Botox and Sunsak Injection

Questions

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us.

Have you had an injection treatment?

To help you recover comfortably, here are the general precautions and the precautions for each treatment. Please check the ones that match your treatment.

General Precautions

No-go for 1 week

Please avoid for 1 week

  • Alcohol
  • Smoking
  • Strenuous exercise that makes you sweat
  • Visiting a jjimjilbang (Korean sauna)
  • Massage
Every day

Sun protection

Always apply sunscreen when you go out.

Contact us right away

If this happens, contact the clinic right away

If severe pain, blisters, skin color changes, or abnormal sensations persist, please contact the clinic immediately.

Skin Boosters

Please check the section for the skin booster you received.

Gouri & Re2O & CELLReDM & Juvelook & Rejuran & Lilide M (릴리이드M) & MCT
  • Sauna, jjimjilbang, massage OK after 2 weeks
  • Washing is allowed on the day
  • Avoid makeup on the day

Saunas, jjimjilbang, and massage are allowed after 2 weeks. You may wash your face on the day, but please avoid makeup on the day.

The injection sites may itch. Please don't scratch them; apply an ice pack instead.

Juvelook Volume & Sculptra
  • Heat and swelling for 3–5 days
  • Bruising possible for about a week
  • Re-treatment after 1 month

Heat and swelling may occur and subside after about 3–5 days. If it is too uncomfortable, apply a cold compress.

Bruising may occur at the treated area for about a week. If there are no particular problems, re-treatment is possible after 1 month.

Fillers

Please check the section for the filler you received.

Fillers (all types)
  • No touching or pressing for 1 month

For 1 month, take care not to touch or press the treated area.

Nose filler & HIKO (하이코)
  • No glasses for 2 weeks

Please do not wear glasses for 2 weeks.

Under-eye filler & Nasolabial fold filler
  • No wide mouth opening for 2 weeks

For 2 weeks, avoid opening your mouth wide (big laughs, yawning, dental treatment).

Botox

Please check the section for the Botox treatment you received.

Botox
  • Do not rub the area
  • Wash and apply makeup after 2–3 hours

Bruising or swelling may occur after treatment.

Do not rub the treated area. It is best to wait about 2–3 hours before washing your face or applying makeup.

Jaw (masseter) Botox
  • Avoid hard foods for now

Please avoid hard foods for a while.

Sunsak Injection (Fat-Dissolving Injection)

Please check this section if you had a Sunsak injection.

Sunsak injection (순삭주사)
  • Wash and apply makeup after 2–3 hours
  • If bruising is severe, do not rub

Bruising or swelling may occur after treatment.

If the bruising is severe, do not rub the area. If not, light massage is recommended.

It is best to wait about 2–3 hours before washing your face or applying makeup.

If you have any concerns during aftercare, please don't wait—let us know. The doctor who treated you will check.
