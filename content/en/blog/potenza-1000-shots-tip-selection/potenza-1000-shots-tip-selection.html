Potenza 1,000 Shots: Price, Effects, and Why the Tip You Choose Changes the Result

Column · Director, Reyeonk Clinic (originally published in Korean)

Is 1,000 shots enough for Potenza, or does the tip you use matter more?

Potenza delivers radiofrequency (RF) energy into the dermis to address enlarged pores, loss of firmness and fine lines at the same time. But even with the same “1,000 shots”, the tip type (pumping tip, needle tip, non-invasive diamond tip) and how energy is distributed across the face change the results you can expect and the downtime. Below we go through how it works, the differences between tips, treatment intervals and pricing, question by question.

3-line summary
  • A 1,000-shot session is the recommended amount for a full-face (whole-face) plan that evens out pores, skin texture and firmness.
  • More important than the shot count is choosing the tip that fits your concern (a pumping tip for drug delivery vs. a non-invasive tip for firmness) and adjusting depth by area.
  • Combining a skin booster or skin botox with a pumping tip delivers the active ingredients directly into the dermis, which improves the synergy for pores and firmness.

How much of the face does 1,000 shots cover?

Potenza delivers RF heat energy to the dermis through the micro-electrodes of its tip, encouraging the skin to regenerate its own collagen and elastin.

When only a local area such as the “butterfly zone” (both cheeks and beside the nose) is the focus, roughly 300–500 shots are typically used. When you plan to refine skin texture and lift across the entire face, the plan is built around 1,000 shots. With 1,000 shots, areas with enlarged pores such as the cheeks and around the nose can be treated with dense overlapping passes, while energy can still be distributed to thinner areas such as around the eyes, the forehead and the jawline.

Does the tip type change the effect, even with the same 1,000 shots?

Potenza has more than 14 dedicated tips. They fall broadly into invasive tips that use fine needles (including the pumping tip) and non-invasive tips (diamond, DDR and others) that sit against the skin surface and deliver RF without needles.

When do Potenza results appear, and how?

New collagen takes about one skin turnover cycle (roughly 3–4 weeks) to form in the dermis after RF heat stimulation.

  • 1–2 weeks after

    Excess oil settles and the skin’s moisture–oil balance improves, so makeup clings better.

  • 3–4 weeks after

    As collagen density gradually builds, enlarged pores tighten and the skin feels firmer.

  • After 3 or more sessions

    Pore-size reduction and fine-line improvement hold more steadily. The recommended number of sessions depends on your skin thickness and pore type.

What is the synergy with a skin booster?

The Potenza pumping tip (CP tip) has a patented structure that uses negative pressure at the moment the needle withdraws to push topically applied drugs into the dermis.

Compared with RF alone, combining a skin booster or skin botox through the pumping tip lets active ingredients be absorbed directly into skin that has been primed for regeneration by heat, which can improve the benefit for pore tightening and inner dryness.

If your skin is thin and low in collagen, so that pores look long and sagging vertically, planning a liquid PCL collagen booster alongside is also a good option. The principle is explained in detail in Gouri skin booster: effects, price, and easing pain, bruising and swelling with Dermashine (Korean).

Should I worry about pain, redness or scabs?

With invasive needle tips you may feel a stinging heat, so numbing cream is applied for enough time before treatment.

After treatment your face may be red or feel hot for 2–3 days, and very thin flakes may appear where the fine needles passed. Do not pick at them; apply moisturiser and sunscreen carefully so the skin recovers smoothly without pigmentation.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

  • Q1. Is 1,000 shots enough for the whole face?

    Yes. For an average adult full face, it is an appropriate number of shots to overlap on the butterfly zone where pores are large and still cover the jawline and forehead evenly.

  • Q2. Is one Potenza session enough to reduce pores?

    After about 3–4 weeks you may notice more even texture, less oiliness and an early change in firmness. If your goal is deeply enlarged pores or acne scars, we recommend at least 3 sessions at 3–4 week intervals.

  • Q3. Are genuine tips used once per person?

    For hygiene and accurate energy delivery, Reyeonk Clinic uses genuine single-use tips as a rule, and you can confirm that a new tip is opened before treatment.

  • Q4. When can I wash my face and wear makeup after Potenza?

    On the day of treatment, rinse gently with lukewarm water only. From the next day you can cleanse with a mild cleanser and wear light makeup.

  • Q5. Small bumps like millet acne appeared after treatment. Is that a side effect?

    During RF heat stimulation and the purging of oil from the pores, temporary folliculitis or small breakouts can appear. Most settle within days with moisturising and soothing care; if they persist, please visit the clinic for soothing care.

  • Q6. Can I combine it with lifting treatments such as Ulthera or Thermage?

    Yes. Pairing a lifting device that tightens the deep fascial layer and lower dermis with Potenza, which refines pores and texture at the surface, can give a three-dimensional anti-ageing effect. For the differences between lifting devices, see Ulthera vs. Thermage and the effect of combining them (Korean).

  • Q7. My skin is sensitive and red. Can I still have Potenza?

    If your skin barrier is thin or redness is marked, we can choose non-invasive tips instead of needles, or lower the energy level, so a consultation beforehand is needed.

  • Q8. What should I avoid after treatment?

    For about a week, avoid saunas, jjimjilbang, strenuous exercise, alcohol and smoking, which can raise skin temperature or trigger inflammation, and moisturise generously.
