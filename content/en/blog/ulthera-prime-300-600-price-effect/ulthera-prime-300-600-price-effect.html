Ulthera 300 vs 600 Shots: Price, Effects, Cheek Hollowing and Pain (Ultherapy PRIME)

Column · Reyeonk Clinic (originally published in Korean)

Is 300 shots enough for Ulthera, or do you need 600?

If a blurring jawline, a double chin, deepening nasolabial folds or sagging cheeks bother you, Ultherapy PRIME is often the first non-surgical lifting treatment people look into. The first question is almost always the shot count. Is 300 shots enough to see a difference? Does 600 shots, at twice the price, give twice the effect? Will it cause cheek hollowing if I have little cheek volume? Reyeonk Clinic in Apgujeong answers each question below: how 300 and 600 shots differ, why prices vary between clinics, cheek hollowing, and pain.

3-line summary
  • 300 shots is usually considered when the focus is on specific concern areas such as the jawline and double chin, and 600 shots when several areas or a wider area are planned together. Twice the shots does not mean twice the effect.
  • Prices differ between clinics because of genuine equipment and tips, the doctor's consultation and treatment design, how shots are distributed by area, the anaesthesia method, and whether event terms and VAT are included.
  • If you have little cheek volume, skin thickness and the subcutaneous fat layer should be checked so shots and depth are adjusted by area. Pain during 600 shots can be managed by discussing anaesthesia options beforehand.

How do Ulthera 300 shots and 600 shots differ in effect?

Ultherapy PRIME is a non-surgical lifting treatment that uses high-intensity focused ultrasound (HIFU) to form thermal coagulation points at specific depths in the skin and trigger collagen regeneration.

What matters is not that more shots are always better, but how precisely the energy is delivered to the areas that need it.

  • Ulthera 300 shots

    300 shots is usually considered when the lifting plan centres on the areas of greatest concern. Shots are concentrated where they are needed rather than spread across the whole face.

    • The jawline has started to blur
    • A double chin and sagging under the chin are the concern
    • Loss of firmness is obvious in one specific area
    • A relatively limited area is to be lifted

  • Ulthera 600 shots

    600 shots is usually considered when sagging in several areas is managed together or a wider area is planned. Its advantage is that energy can be distributed across more areas and layers than 300 shots.

    • Loss of firmness appears in both the cheeks and jawline
    • The double chin and facial contour are both a concern
    • Different areas need treatment at different depths
    • Overall firmness across the face is the goal

Depending on face size and skin condition, even 300 shots can cover a wide area, and the actual range depends on the doctor's assessment and design.

600 shots is also not necessarily twice as effective as 300 shots. Even with 600 shots, it can be hard to get the result you expect if fat volume, skin thickness and the direction of sagging are not taken into account.

The point is not which of 300 or 600 shots is better, but how many shots your own face needs. If your skin is thin or you have little cheek volume, setting an appropriate area and depth matters more than applying many shots to areas that do not need them.

What is different about Ultherapy PRIME compared with the original Ulthera?

Ultherapy PRIME keeps the ultrasound lifting principle of the original Ulthera while improving how the practitioner sees the skin layers and how convenient the system is to use. The biggest difference is the advance in real-time ultrasound imaging.

  • 1. A clearer view of the skin layers

    A screen about 35% larger and an ultrasound imaging field twice as wide make the structures beneath the skin easier to observe. Images can be viewed down to a depth of 8 mm, which helps in planning treatment around the condition of your skin.

  • 2. More precise, tailored lifting design

    By checking skin layers and tissue on the ultrasound image, treatment can be planned so that energy is delivered at an appropriate depth.

  • 3. An improved treatment environment

    Data-processing performance is up to 10 times higher than the previous device and, according to the manufacturer, the speed of the treatment platform has also improved.

That does not mean processing 10 times faster cuts treatment time to a tenth or reduces pain to a tenth. The real advantage of Ultherapy PRIME is not speed in itself but being able to plan a tailored lift while looking inside the skin.

Can 600 shots of Ulthera cause cheek hollowing?

Cheek hollowing is one of the biggest worries for people considering Ulthera. If you have little cheek volume or the area below the cheekbone looks sunken, extra caution is needed.

Ulthera delivers thermal energy to tissue beneath the skin, so treatment that ignores the amount of fat and the skin thickness in each area can raise concern about unwanted hollowing or changes in contour. Rather than simply applying many shots, the following should be considered.

  • Distinguishing areas with little cheek volume from areas with marked sagging
  • Checking skin thickness and the subcutaneous fat layer
  • Choosing a tip of the appropriate depth for each area
  • Avoiding excessive energy on areas that do not need it
  • A design that considers the three-dimensional contour of the whole face

The real-time imaging of Ultherapy PRIME helps in treating while checking the skin layers. However, having the imaging function does not mean side effects such as cheek hollowing can be completely prevented.

How painful is Ulthera, and is 600 shots manageable?

As energy is delivered to the deep layers of the skin, Ulthera can cause a tingling or deep, dull ache. How much pain you feel depends on your sensitivity, the treatment area and the energy settings.

Because 600 shots covers a wide area and can take longer, it is best to discuss pain management beforehand. At Reyeonk Clinic, several methods of pain relief are considered according to your condition and treatment plan.

  • Ultrasound-assisted local anaesthesia

    A method that uses an ultrasound device to assist local anaesthesia, aiming to reduce discomfort during treatment. How well the anaesthetic is delivered and how much pain relief you get differ from person to person.

  • IV sedation and inhaled sedation

    If you are sensitive to pain, applicable methods such as IV sedation or inhaled sedation can be discussed after the doctor's assessment. Every anaesthesia method has its own risks and precautions, so the choice should consider your health and the extent of treatment.

If Ulthera alone is not enough, what about the combined Ul-Thermage lift?

Ultherapy PRIME aims to improve sagging and contour in the deeper layers of the skin. If fine lines, skin texture and loss of firmness in the dermis are also a concern, combining it with another energy-based treatment can be considered. The combination Reyeonk Clinic typically offers is Ul-Thermage (Ultherapy PRIME + Thermage FLX).

  • Ultherapy PRIME

    Aims to lift the deep layers of the skin using high-intensity focused ultrasound.

  • Thermage FLX

    Uses radiofrequency energy to help improve skin firmness and fine lines.

Combining the two lets you plan a lift that takes different skin layers and concerns into account. That does not mean having both is more effective for everyone. Depending on your skin, a single treatment may be more suitable.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

  • Q1. How many Ulthera shots are right for my face?

    Skin thickness, degree of sagging, fat volume and treatment area matter more than face size. If only a specific area such as the jawline or double chin is a concern, a focused treatment of around 300 shots can be considered. If sagging appears in several areas such as the cheeks and jawline, or a wide area is planned, 600 shots can be considered. 300 and 600 are not absolute standards, and some people need fewer or more. At Reyeonk Clinic, the doctor examines your skin and facial contour in person and decides the appropriate number of shots and treatment area.

  • Q2. Why do Ulthera 300-shot and 600-shot prices differ so much between clinics?

    Because, beyond the shot count, the doctor's technique, anaesthesia, additional care and event packages differ. Even for the same Ultherapy PRIME 600 shots, the amount can vary with the use of genuine tips, area-by-area design, whether the doctor performs the treatment personally, and extra services. Rather than choosing only because it is cheap, check the exact shot count and what is included. For event prices in particular, check whether VAT, anaesthesia fees and additional care costs are included.

  • Q3. I have little cheek volume. Can Ulthera cause cheek hollowing?

    The possibility of cheek hollowing cannot be ruled out entirely, so a careful treatment design is needed. If you have little cheek volume and the area under the cheekbone is sunken, applying the same intensity and shot count to the whole face may not be appropriate. Where needed, treatment on the central cheek can be reduced or avoided and the plan adjusted to focus on the jawline or areas where sagging is confirmed. Having little cheek volume does not by itself mean you must avoid Ulthera, but it is important to check the fat volume and skin layers thoroughly beforehand.

  • Q4. When do the effects of Ulthera 600 shots appear?

    Generally, gradual change can be expected over several weeks to 2–3 months. As collagen remodelling proceeds after the tissue response to ultrasound energy, changes in firmness and lifting can be felt. The timing and duration of the effect vary with each person's skin condition and degree of ageing.

  • Q5. Is Ulthera 600 shots always more effective than 300 shots?

    No. 600 shots can distribute energy over a wider area, but twice the shots does not mean twice the effect. For people with thin skin or mild sagging, 300 shots may be more suitable, while for sagging in several areas 600 shots can be advantageous for planning the treatment range.
