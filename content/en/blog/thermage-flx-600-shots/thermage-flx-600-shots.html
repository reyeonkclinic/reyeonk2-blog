Does your skin feel less firm than it used to?

Fine lines you notice every time you look in the mirror, a face line that is slowly losing definition, skin that is losing firmness and starting to sag. Skin aging does not start overnight. Here is a summary of why many people choose Thermage FLX 600 shots, the effects, how it differs from Oligio and Density, pain, and sleep anesthesia.

Why skin loses firmness

In general, from the mid-20s the balance between collagen production and breakdown in the skin begins to change. Changes in collagen and elastic fibers in the dermis, the layer that supports the skin, can lead to fine lines and sagging.

Thermage FLX, a radiofrequency (RF) lifting treatment, is one that many people look into to manage these changes.

What is Thermage FLX?

Thermage FLX is a non-invasive treatment that delivers radiofrequency (RF) energy to skin tissue. It is a 4th-generation device with U.S. FDA clearance and has been used for more than 20 years on the eye area and face wrinkles, making it a representative RF lifting device.

The RF energy delivered to the tissue produces heat, and through the following collagen remodeling process, improvement in skin firmness and tightening can be expected. It also involves no surgical incision of the skin.

  • Improved skin firmness
  • Softer fine lines around the eyes and face
  • Skin tightening
  • Helps improve skin texture

Effects on pore size or deeper wrinkles may be limited depending on skin condition, and results are not the same for everyone.

Why do we recommend Thermage FLX 600 shots?

The question people ask most when researching Thermage is about 600 shots. That is because 600 shots is a commonly chosen setup for managing firmness across the whole face.

  • You can design the whole face at once

    It is a shot count that lets you plan the whole face at once: loss of firmness around the cheeks and cheekbones, fine lines around the nasolabial folds, and sagging around the mouth and jawline.

  • Energy can be divided and planned by area

    Thin and thick areas of skin, and areas with more or less fat, all behave differently. What matters more than the shot count itself is energy control and treatment design that take skin thickness, firmness and facial fat into account.

  • It also fits genuine tip sizes

    Genuine tips come in 300, 600 and 900 shots, with one tip per person. If a clinic offers split sessions such as 100 or 150 shots, or treats beyond the 2-hour time lock, you may suspect a reprocessed tip.

At Reyeonk Clinic, the doctor consults with you in person, checks your skin, and plans the treatment area and energy level. Even for the same 600 shots, we do not design every patient the same way.

How painful is Thermage?

Because Thermage delivers RF heat, you may feel heat, a prickling sensation, or a burning feeling. Pain depends on skin condition, energy settings and personal sensitivity, and many people find it less painful than Ulthera.

However, 600 shots is a considerable number, so people who are sensitive to pain may feel discomfort during treatment. If pain is a worry, discuss anesthesia options thoroughly before treatment.

Thermage sleep anesthesia vs Airnox

  • Ultrasound-assisted local anesthesia

    A method that uses an ultrasound device to help the topical anesthetic reach the skin, and may be considered depending on your condition and treatment settings to reduce the burden of pain.

  • Airnox (smile gas)

    A sedation and pain-relief system in which you breathe a mix of nitrous oxide and oxygen, helping ease discomfort while you stay awake. It requires no fasting, no abstaining from alcohol or smoking, can be done on the same day, and you can go home after resting 10 to 20 minutes. It may not be possible for people with lung disease or breathing difficulties.

  • Sleep anesthesia

    A method in which you sleep during treatment using a sedative, which people who are sensitive to pain or find a long treatment hard may consider after evaluation by the doctor. Reyeonk Clinic performs it only in an operating room equipped with safety and monitoring equipment such as oxygen respiratory support and oxygen saturation measurement.

    Prior consultation required for sleep anesthesia · same-day booking not available

On the day of sleep anesthesia, you need to fast for at least 4 hours and avoid alcohol and smoking for at least a day. Because we also need to check your medications, any illnesses, allergies and past anesthesia experience, please tell us when booking that you want sleep anesthesia. After treatment you may feel dizziness, headache or nausea, so you rest in a private room before going home.

When do Thermage effects appear and how long do they last?

Some people feel their skin tighten relatively soon after treatment, but collagen remodeling takes time. Changes in firmness can generally be expected over several months, and the degree and timing differ from person to person.

Duration depends on skin condition, degree of aging and lifestyle, and many people expect improvement lasting about 6 months to 1 year. A 1 to 2 year effect is not guaranteed for everyone, and since skin aging continues after treatment, sun protection and steady care matter.

  • Retreatment interval: 1 to 1.5 years

    We recommend repeating Thermage at an interval of 1 year to 1 year 6 months.

  • If firmness drops quickly, Density Classic at 6 months

    If your skin tends to lose firmness quickly, following Thermage with Density Classic at around 6 months may help extend how long the results last.

Does Thermage cause hollow cheeks?

Thermage does not affect fat, so the risk of hollow cheeks is very low. That makes it a good lifting option even for people worried about losing fat in the cheeks.

Facial structure and fat volume differ from person to person, so please confirm the details in a consultation with the doctor.

Why combine Thermage with a skin booster?

Thermage on its own is fine, but when it is combined with skin boosters such as Gouri or Re2O, the skin is nourished and its layers are strengthened, which helps firmness and skin texture.

Ingredients and characteristics vary by skin booster, so we recommend choosing, with your doctor, the product that suits your skin condition.

Side effects and precautions before treatment

Thermage is non-invasive, but the possibility of side effects is not zero. Temporary redness, swelling, pain and a feeling of heat may appear after treatment, and rarely blisters, burns, changes in sensation or surface irregularities can occur.

For safe treatment, tip contact, energy settings and the characteristics of the treated area must be carefully considered, so a consultation and treatment by an experienced doctor are important.
