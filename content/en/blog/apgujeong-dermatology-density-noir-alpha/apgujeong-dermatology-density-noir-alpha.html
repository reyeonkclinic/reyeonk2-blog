Apgujeong Dermatology: What Sets the 2nd-Generation Density Noir Alpha Apart, and What to Know Before You Choose

Column · Reyeonk Clinic (originally published in Korean)

Look into Density treatments and you will see the names “Noir” and “Alpha” side by side.

The names alone do not tell you what is different. This article explains what the 2nd-generation Density Noir Alpha is, and what to check before you choose.

3-line summary
  • The 2nd-generation Density Noir is an upgraded version of the original Density device.
  • Alpha uses two types of radiofrequency energy, monopolar and bipolar, to work on the surface and the deeper layers of the skin at the same time.
  • Noir offers Gentle Mode and Infinite Mode. Which suits you is best confirmed at a consultation.

This column is a translation of an article originally written in Korean by Reyeonk Clinic.

From Density to Noir: what changed?

Density is a lifting device that uses radiofrequency energy. The 2nd-generation Density Noir is an upgraded version of the original Density. Because it is an improvement within the same line of devices, if you have heard of the original Density you can think of Noir as its next generation.

What is Alpha?

Noir Alpha uses two types of radiofrequency energy together: monopolar and bipolar. Monopolar tends to act on relatively deeper layers and bipolar on relatively shallower ones, so using both allows lifting that works on the surface and the deeper layers of the skin at the same time.

Noir’s two modes: Gentle Mode and Infinite Mode

  • Gentle Mode

    Lets you adjust how deeply the skin is pressed, so contact conditions can be matched to each area.

  • Infinite Mode

    Delivers output continuously within the set parameters, spreading energy evenly over wide areas.

Which mode suits you depends on the treatment area and your skin condition, so we recommend confirming it at a consultation.

What to check about a device that combines two types of energy

  • How much energy goes to which layer depends on your skin thickness and the degree of sagging.

  • Even with the same device, the treatment area and intensity settings are designed differently for each person.

  • That is why it matters more whether the clinic explains “how it applies to my skin” than which device it uses.

If any of these sound like you, ask at your consultation

  • You feel your jawline is not what it used to be

  • One type of lifting has left you wanting more

  • You wonder how it differs from other lifting treatments such as Ulthera or Thermage

Whether to have the treatment, and how, depends on your skin condition, so we explain the details at your consultation.
