Bothered by both enlarged pores and loss of firmness? Is VirtueRF the right treatment?

This is Reyeonk Clinic near Apgujeong Rodeo Station. Here are 20 questions people ask most when looking into pore treatment and VirtueRF lifting, from how it works to sessions, discomfort, aftercare and how pricing is decided.

At a glance
  • VirtueRF is a microneedle radiofrequency (RF) treatment that delivers energy to a target depth in the skin with fine needles.
  • What matters is less the needle itself than how the energy is designed to reach the target depth, which is why skin texture and firmness are assessed together.
  • Pores cannot be removed completely; the goal is to make them less noticeable.
  • Potenza and VirtueRF are both microneedle RF, so the question is whether it suits your skin, not which is better.
  • Sessions, discomfort, recovery and cost depend on your skin and the settings used and are confirmed at consultation.

What is VirtueRF? How does the research summarise it?

A 2026 review of studies using radiofrequency microneedling for facial rejuvenation (Kumar et al., J Cosmet Dermatol, PMID 41947517) summarised it as effective and well tolerated. In a 2023 study of Korean participants (Suh et al., J Cosmet Dermatol, PMID 36718800), fine lines and sagging improved after temperature-controlled microneedle RF treatment, and biopsies showed increased collagen and elastic fibre formation.

These results belong to the people studied, and actual results vary between individuals.

10 questions about pore treatment

  • 1. Can enlarged pores really be reduced with dermatology treatments?

    Pores are a normal skin structure that releases oil, so they cannot be removed completely. What can be aimed for is improving skin texture and the tissue around the pores so that they look less noticeable. The degree of improvement varies with why the pores enlarged (oil production, repeated irritation, loss of firmness) and with your skin.

  • 2. Which laser works best for pore reduction?

    It is hard to say one device suits every pore. Microneedle RF (Potenza, VirtueRF) and picosecond laser approaches (PicoSure Fraxel) are among those used, and the right choice depends on pore shape, skin thickness and scarring. Examining the skin first to identify the cause is what matters.

  • 3. Potenza or VirtueRF for pores: which is better?

    Both are microneedle radiofrequency (RF) devices. Tip configurations, how energy is delivered and available settings differ, so rather than ranking them by name, the question is whether one suits your skin.

  • 4. Are nose pores and cheek pores treated differently?

    They can be. Pores on the nose often centre on oil control and exfoliation because the oil glands are active, while vertically stretched pores on the cheeks often involve loss of firmness, so skin texture and the deeper skin layer are considered together. Different areas of the same face can be approached differently.

  • 5. How many sessions are needed before pore treatment shows an effect?

    It depends on pore size, firmness, oil production and scarring. Some plans involve around three repeated sessions, but not everyone follows the same number; the interval and number of sessions are adjusted according to how your skin responds.

  • 6. Can pores become enlarged again after treatment?

    Even after the skin improves, oil production, ageing and outside irritation keep acting, so changes can occur over time. Avoiding forceful squeezing and keeping up basic care such as sun protection, cleansing and moisturising can help. How long the result lasts varies between individuals.

  • 7. Can pore reduction and skin firmness be treated at the same time?

    Microneedle RF delivers energy to a target depth to induce collagen remodelling, so it is a treatment that considers pores, skin texture and firmness together. A 2026 review of RF microneedling for facial rejuvenation summarised it as effective and well tolerated, and a 2023 study of Korean participants reported improved fine lines and sagging with increased collagen and elastic fibres on biopsy. These results apply to the people studied, and effects vary between individuals.

  • 8. How long do discomfort and redness last after pore laser treatment?

    Needle-based treatment can cause stinging and a sense of heat, and temporary redness, swelling and small scabs may follow. How long they last depends on intensity, settings and your skin's response, so it is most accurate to be advised at consultation based on your own skin. If discomfort is a concern, anaesthesia options can be discussed with the doctor.

  • 9. How much does pore treatment cost and how many sessions are needed?

    Cost and the number of sessions depend on the device, tip, treatment area, number of shots and whether treatments are combined. Please check the current pricing at your consultation.

  • 10. What should I check when choosing a pore clinic in Apgujeong?

    Check five things: (1) whether the cause of your pores is examined first, (2) whether more than one suitable option is explained rather than a single device, (3) whether the reasons for the depth and energy settings are explained, (4) whether discomfort, recovery and side effects are explained in advance, and (5) who personally handles you from consultation to follow-up. At Reyeonk Clinic, our doctors personally handle consultation, treatment and follow-up, as we have since opening in 2009.

10 questions about VirtueRF lifting

  • 11. How does VirtueRF lifting improve skin firmness?

    VirtueRF is a microneedle RF treatment that delivers radiofrequency energy to a target depth with fine needles. What matters is less the needle itself than how the energy is designed to reach the target depth; the skin responds where the energy is delivered, inducing collagen remodelling.

  • 12. Does VirtueRF also work for pore reduction?

    Because VirtueRF considers skin texture and firmness together, it can be considered when enlarged pores and rough texture are the concern. Pores caused by oil, pores with scarring and pores stretched by lost firmness may need different approaches, and the degree of effect varies between individuals.

  • 13. What is the difference between VirtueRF and Potenza?

    Both are microneedle RF. Potenza is characterised by its range of tips and modes, while VirtueRF adjusts energy depth and conditions to the skin and is used to address texture and firmness together. Rather than treating one as always better, the choice is made by looking at pore condition, skin thickness and scarring.

  • 14. VirtueRF or Thermage: which suits skin firmness better?

    They work differently. VirtueRF is a microneedle method that delivers RF into the skin with fine needles, while Thermage delivers RF energy without needles. Which suits you depends on whether sagging is the main concern or texture, pores and fine lines matter as well, and they may sometimes be planned together. For Thermage, see our Thermage FLX 600 shots article.

  • 15. How many VirtueRF sessions are needed to feel a difference?

    It depends on your skin, how pronounced the concern is and your goal, so no single number applies. After checking your skin at consultation, we plan treatment and adjust the number and interval according to how your skin responds.

  • 16. When do VirtueRF results appear?

    Right after treatment there may be redness and swelling, and collagen remodelling proceeds over time, so changes in texture and firmness are noticed gradually after recovery. Timing and degree vary with your skin and the settings used.

  • 17. Does VirtueRF work on fine lines around the eyes and under-eye firmness?

    A study of Korean participants reported improved fine lines and sagging after temperature-controlled microneedle RF treatment. The skin around the eyes is thin, though, so energy settings and treatment area need more careful selection, and effects vary between individuals.

  • 18. How painful is VirtueRF and is anaesthesia needed?

    Because fine needles are used, there may be stinging and a sense of heat, and how much you feel varies between people and areas. If discomfort is a concern, an anaesthesia method suited to your skin and treatment plan can be decided with the doctor at consultation.

  • 19. How much does VirtueRF cost and does it vary by area?

    It varies with the treatment area and extent, the number of sessions and whether treatments are combined. We confirm the range your skin needs at consultation and explain the current pricing then.

  • 20. When can I wash my face, wear makeup and resume daily life after VirtueRF?

    After microneedle treatment we may advise avoiding makeup for a period so the skin barrier can recover. The exact timing depends on treatment intensity and how your skin responds, so please follow the instructions you are given. As a general principle, use sun protection and moisturiser, and avoid exfoliation and irritating products.
