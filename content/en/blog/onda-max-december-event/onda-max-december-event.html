Onda MAX: face slimming and lifting at once

Onda MAX is a combination treatment that performs Onda lifting and the Sunsak injection (fat-dissolving injection) together.

Onda lifting handles fat reduction and skin firmness with microwave energy, and the Sunsak injection breaks down unwanted fat cells once more, aiming at a slimmer, firmer face and body line.

Onda lifting effects: how does it work?

Onda lifting is a non-surgical treatment that delivers microwave energy deep into the skin to tidy up unwanted fat and stimulate collagen production, aiming at firmer, sagging skin.

During treatment you usually feel only mild warmth, and although it varies, most people can return to daily life straight away. Swelling, tightness, itching, a heavy feeling in the muscle or an uncomfortable feeling in facial expressions can occasionally occur, and they are mostly temporary.

What makes the Sunsak injection different

The Sunsak injection is Reyeonk Clinic's fat-dissolving injection: based on our accumulated experience, drugs that work on fat breakdown are mixed at a high concentration and given in a small volume. Because the volume is small, bruising and swelling tend to be mild, and we do not use steroids or PPC.

For more about the Sunsak injection, see the double chin Sunsak injection page (Korean).

Are more joules always better?

A higher number of joules is not automatically better. We do not recommend adding energy when there is not much fat, and if energy concentrates on one area it can cause asymmetry or an uneven surface later.

So the event is built around 80,000 J as the base, and the actual number of joules and the energy are decided by the doctor at the consultation, after checking your fat volume, skin condition and face and body lines.

Aftercare

  • Avoid cold water washing and ice packs for 2 days

    Because this treatment works with heat stimulation, avoid cold stimulation for about 2 days after treatment.

  • Always use sunscreen when you go out

    Skin can be sensitive to irritation after treatment.

  • Avoid exfoliation for about a week

    We recommend skipping scrubs, peels and other exfoliation for about a week.

Onda lifting: 17 frequently asked questions

  • What kind of energy does Onda use?

    Onda uses microwave energy, which acts on the subcutaneous fat layer and the dermis.

  • How is it different from radiofrequency (RF) or ultrasound (HIFU)?

    RF and HIFU are specialised for collagen stimulation and focused energy respectively. Onda is characterised by acting on the fat layer while keeping damage to the skin surface to a minimum.

  • Can it be done on both the face and body?

    Yes, on both. Depending on the area, a 3 mm or 7 mm handpiece is chosen, or the two are combined.

  • Is anesthesia needed?

    Smart cooling reduces discomfort, so treatment is usually done without anesthesia. If you are sensitive to pain, a numbing cream can be used.

  • Does it really break down fat?

    It works by reducing the size of fat cells and destroying some of them. The change is smaller than liposuction, but it can be expected to help in areas such as the double chin. Results vary between individuals.

  • Can I have it together with Ulthera or Thermage?

    Yes, they can be combined. The combination and order are decided after a consultation, according to your skin condition.

  • Are the effects permanent?

    Destroyed fat cells do not regenerate, but your line can change again with weight changes or ageing. For that reason we recommend repeat treatment every 2 to 3 months.

  • How painful is the treatment?

    We describe it as a level that can be done without anesthesia. It can feel uncomfortable at higher energy, so the doctor adjusts it in real time based on your response.

  • Is it possible with acne or sensitive skin?

    It is generally possible because damage to the skin surface is small, but if you have severe active acne or inflammation, it is better to postpone.

  • Can I use Onda for maintenance after other lifting treatments?

    Yes. We recommend maintenance at intervals of 2 to 3 months.

  • How much does Onda cost?

    It depends on the area and range, and the exact amount is given after a consultation with the doctor. As a December event, Onda lifting 80,000 J + face Sunsak injection is KRW 580,000 (VAT excluded), and Mint Lifting + Onda lifting + face Sunsak injection is KRW 1,480,000 (VAT excluded).

  • What is the difference between Onda and InMode?

    Onda uses microwaves, and InMode uses radiofrequency (RF). Onda is characterised by minimising irritation of the skin surface.

  • How long do the effects of Onda last?

    It varies between individuals, but we usually say about 4 to 5 months. Consistent care and repeat treatment help.

  • Can I go back to daily life right after Onda?

    Yes. Slight swelling can occur, but it usually settles quickly.

  • Who is Onda especially recommended for?

    We recommend it for people who want to improve fat and firmness together, who want treatment without downtime, or who are sensitive to irritation of the skin surface.

  • A few days have passed since my Onda treatment and I see no effect.

    Fat cells need time to be gradually cleared from the body, so it is too early to judge after a few days. Changes appear gradually and vary between individuals.

  • How many kJ is Onda performed at?

    It is decided according to the area and your skin condition. We usually guide about 100 kJ for a face, about 120 kJ for a man's face and about 150 kJ for the body, and this event is built around 80,000 J (80 kJ). The final energy is decided by the doctor at the consultation.
