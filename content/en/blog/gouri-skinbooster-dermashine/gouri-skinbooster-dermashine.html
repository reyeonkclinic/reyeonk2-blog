What is the Gouri skinbooster?

Gouri's main ingredient is PCL (polycaprolactone). PCL has long been used in dissolvable sutures and some collagen boosters, but earlier products were fine particles. Gouri turns this PCL into a particle-free liquid.

ItemParticle-type PCLGouri (liquid PCL)
FormFine particlesParticle-free liquid
Distribution after injectionConcern that particles may gather in one placeSpreads evenly without gathering
Lumps and nodulesA common worryRelatively less concern

"Relatively less" does not mean "none." Please check the possibility during your consultation.

Does it inject collagen, or build it?

Earlier skinboosters often injected collagen directly into the skin. Gouri works differently. Products like this are called biostimulators. They enter the skin and encourage the body to produce its own collagen.

Injected collagen is absorbed over time, whereas collagen your body builds itself becomes part of the skin's structure. That is the difference. For more on the types of skinboosters, see the guide on skinbooster types, prices and pain.

How do Gouri results appear?

Because the collagen the body builds becomes part of the skin's structure over time, this treatment aims for changes that appear from the base of the skin.

When you feel results and how long they last varies from person to person. It is best understood as a treatment where you see changes that develop over time, rather than the look right after treatment.

  • Skin density
  • Elasticity
  • Fine lines

How are pain, bruising and swelling reduced?

Pain is the biggest reason people hesitate over Gouri. Reyeonk Clinic performs Gouri with the dermashine method to help reduce worries about pain, bruising and swelling.

What is the dermashine method?

  • 9-pin multi-needle

    Injects into 9 points at once per shot, so the treatment is quick.

  • Less pain and bruising

    Generally less pain and bruising than hand injection.

  • Negative-pressure adhesion

    The skin is held with negative pressure during injection, which reduces how much solution flows out.

How does it work with lifting treatments?

Lifting devices such as Ulthera and Thermage pull sagging areas upward. Gouri works by strengthening the skin's foundation. Even if you lift sagging areas, results are hard to keep if the skin is thin and lacks strength, so planning both together can help cover each other's gaps.

Whether to combine them and in what order depends on your skin condition, so please decide during consultation. For the differences between lifting devices, see our article on Ulthera vs. Thermage and their combined effects.

Frequently asked questions

  • Q1. How is Gouri different from other skinboosters?

    Instead of injecting collagen directly, Gouri is a biostimulator that encourages the body to make collagen itself. For the differences between skinbooster types, see our guide on skinbooster types, prices and pain.

  • Q2. Does Gouri hurt a lot?

    Pain varies between people. Reyeonk Clinic performs Gouri with the dermashine method to help reduce the burden of pain. If you are sensitive to pain, please tell us during your consultation.

  • Q3. I'm worried about bruising and swelling. Can dermashine reduce them?

    Dermashine helps reduce worries about pain, bruising and swelling. Slight redness or minor swelling at the injection sites may occur, and the degree varies between people. Please follow the aftercare instructions.

  • Q4. Are there concerns about lumps or nodules?

    Gouri is a particle-free liquid, so it spreads evenly instead of gathering in one spot, and the lump and nodule concerns seen with particle products are relatively lower. Relatively lower does not mean none, so please check this thoroughly during consultation.

  • Q5. Can I combine it with lifting treatments such as Ulthera or Thermage?

    Lifting devices pull sagging areas upward, while Gouri is described as strengthening the skin's foundation. Whether to combine them and in what order depends on your skin condition, so please decide during consultation.

  • Q6. How many treatments do I need?

    It depends on your skin condition and the changes you want. We generally recommend 3 sessions at 4 to 6 week intervals.

  • Q7. Should Gouri be diluted to 5cc?

    The manufacturer recommends diluting to 5cc. People ask whether further dilution reduces the effect. For example, receiving Juvelook and Rejuran together can create a synergy, and Reyeonk Clinic injects growth factors together with Gouri to enhance firmness and skin texture.

  • Q8. What is the difference between a skinbooster and a moisture injection?

    A simple moisture injection uses hyaluronic acid without cross-linking, which can hold water, to keep moisture in the dermis. Modern skinboosters use bioactive ingredients such as allogeneic dermis, PDRN, PN, PCL, PDLLA and PLA to promote skin regeneration itself, so they can improve the skin at a deeper level.

  • Q9. How long do skinbooster results last?

    It depends on the product. Rejuran lasts 1 to 6 months, Rejuo lasts 3 to 6 months, and Sculptra lasts more than 12 months. Results build up with regular series treatments.

  • Q10. Do skinbooster treatments hurt?

    A numbing cream is applied before treatment to minimize pain. Glow injections cause slight pricking, and combining with RF microneedling may cause some discomfort. When a machine is used, the treatment can be done with no pain at all.

  • Q11. Which skinbooster is right for me?

    It depends on your skin condition and goals. For immediate radiance, Rejuran is suggested; for deep regeneration, the stem cell MCT or allogeneic dermis Rejuo; for combined improvement, Gouri and others. The best ingredient is chosen after consultation.

  • Q12. Can I return to daily life right after a skinbooster?

    Most people can return the same day. There may be slight redness or small dot marks after treatment, which usually disappear within 2 to 3 days. Washing your face and light makeup are possible on the same day.

  • Q13. Does a skinbooster improve wrinkles?

    Yes, the collagen-regenerating effect of skinboosters improves fine lines and skin texture. However, deep wrinkles or sagging may need additional treatments such as lifting or fillers.

  • Q14. Are there aftercare instructions after a skinbooster?

    Avoid intense exercise, saunas and alcohol for 24 hours after treatment. Keep the treated area clean, minimize direct sun exposure and always use sunscreen. Makeup is possible from the day after treatment.
