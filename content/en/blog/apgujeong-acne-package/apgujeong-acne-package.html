Acne treatment starts with getting the order right.

This column is a translation of an article originally written in Korean by the director of Reyeonk Clinic. Links marked “Korean” lead to Korean-language pages.

Which procedure you get, and when, can change how acne responds. The Reyeonk Clinic acne package starts with a look at your skin, then sets the order of each session with you. The 14 sessions are laid out below.

Summary
  • The package runs for three months, one session a week, 14 sessions in total, with treatment and care alternating.
  • It begins with concerns that clear up relatively quickly, moves on to scars and pores, and finishes with skin texture. The order adjusts to your skin.
  • The director handles everything personally, from the first consultation to the final session.

Leave the order to us, like omakase

Omakase (おまかせ) means "leaving it to the chef," a Japanese way of dining where the chef picks the dishes. The Reyeonk Clinic acne package works in a similar way: we look at your skin first, then work out the order of each session together.

On your first visit, we take photos with the Mark View skin diagnostic device, then talk with the director. A consultation usually takes 20 to 30 minutes, and we go over your skin history and what is bothering you now.

What the acne package includes

The package lasts three months, with one session a week for 14 sessions. Treatments (laser procedures) and care sessions alternate, seven of each.

Treatment flow by session

  • Sessions 1 and 3: Starting with what clears up quickly

    We start with pigmentation and spots (Pico laser), moles and flat warts (MCL31), and sebaceous hyperplasia and syringomas (Agnes radiofrequency needle). If your acne is more severe, we may also suggest medication. Gold PTT is an optional treatment, and you decide it with the medical team after a consultation.

  • Sessions 5 and 7: Working on scars and pores

    If you have scars, we may recommend ablative fractional laser (MCL31). Plan on 3 to 4 days with no outside commitments afterward, and we will help you schedule around it. If enlarged pores bother you, we use Potenza (microneedle radiofrequency).

  • Sessions 9 and 11: Extra treatments after a team consultation

    Instead of high-intensity treatments, we refine skin texture with non-ablative fractional laser (PicoSure fractional) or Wikouno peeling. The exact treatments can change based on the medical team's judgment.

  • Session 13: Final precision care

    The treatment is chosen based on your scar type and skin condition.

    · Rolling scars: TriFill Pro (air lifting + Juvelook)
    · Ice-pick scars: TCA dot peel
    · Firmness and texture: Dermaatome + skin botox (topical application)
    · Redness: Synergy Focus laser (vascular laser)

* In more severe cases, treatments that need longer recovery, like TriFill Pro or TCA peeling, may be done earlier in the course. Some treatments take two to three months to show their effect.

Main treatments used in the package

  • Pigmentation and spots

    Pico laser: removal of spots, melasma and pigmented lesions

  • Moles, warts and sebaceous lesions

    MCL31: moles, flat warts, ablative fractional laser / Agnes radiofrequency needle: sebaceous hyperplasia, syringomas

  • Acne and sebaceous gland care

    Gold PTT: sebaceous gland reduction, improvement of inflammatory acne (optional) / Medication: regulating sebum production, reducing inflammation

  • Scars and pores

    Ablative fractional laser (MCL31): deep scars, skin regeneration / Potenza (microneedle radiofrequency): pores, skin texture, sebaceous gland reduction / TriFill Pro: rolling scars / TCA dot peel: ice-pick scars

  • Texture and tone

    Non-ablative fractional laser (PicoSure fractional): texture, tone-up / Wikouno peel: exfoliation, refining texture / Dermaatome + skin botox: firmness, texture

  • Redness and blood vessels

    Synergy Focus laser: vascular lesions, flushing, redness

After the 14 sessions

Throughout the course, we check your skin together every week and talk about where it is heading. Even after the final care session in week 14, we keep checking your skin and talk through any care you may still need.

Frequently asked questions

  • How long does the acne package take?

    It's a three-month course, one session a week, 14 sessions in total.

  • How do treatments and care sessions alternate?

    They alternate. Odd-numbered sessions are treatments and even-numbered ones are care, seven of each.

  • How long is the consultation?

    Usually 20 to 30 minutes. On your first visit we take photos with Mark View, then talk with the director.

  • Do the treatments hurt a lot?

    Some need anesthesia and some do not. How much it hurts varies from person to person, so we allow plenty of time for anesthesia to work.

  • Can I go about my day after treatment?

    Recovery time depends on the treatment. After ablative fractional laser, you may want to keep 3 to 4 days free of outside plans, so it helps to arrange ahead. We will explain the detailed aftercare during your consultation.

  • When will I see results?

    Some treatments take two to three months to show results. Results vary from person to person.

  • If my acne is severe, will I also be prescribed medication?

    Medication may be recommended. It is prescribed after a consultation with a doctor. Gold PTT is optional and is decided with the medical team during consultation.

  • Will the director personally perform my treatments?

    Yes. The director handles everything, from consultation through the final session.

  • How can I find out the price?

    We do not publish prices. The director explains them in person during the consultation.
