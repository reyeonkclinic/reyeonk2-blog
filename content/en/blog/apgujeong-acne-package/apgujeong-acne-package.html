Let us guide the order of your acne treatment.

This column is a translation of an article originally written in Korean by the director of Reyeonk Clinic. Links marked “Korean” lead to Korean-language pages.

With acne care, the procedure you choose and when you receive it can shape the result. The acne package at Reyeonk Clinic in Apgujeong works by first assessing your skin and then deciding the order of each session together. Here is a clear overview of the 14-session structure and how it progresses.

Summary
  • The acne package runs for three months, once a week, for 14 sessions in total, alternating treatment and care.
  • It starts with items that can be cleared quickly, then moves on to scars and pores, then skin texture, adjusted to your skin.
  • The director personally handles everything from consultation and treatment to final care.

Leave the order to us, like omakase

Omakase (おまかせ) is a Japanese dining style that means "leaving it to the chef": you let the chef choose the dishes. The Reyeonk Clinic acne package works the same way. We first assess your skin, then decide the session-by-session order together.

On your first visit, we take photos with the Mark View skin diagnostic device and meet the director for a consultation. The consultation usually takes about 20 to 30 minutes, and we review your skin history and current concerns together.

What the acne package includes

The package runs for three months, once a week, for 14 sessions in total. Treatments (laser procedures) and care sessions alternate, with seven of each.

Treatment flow by session

  • Sessions 1 and 3: Start with what can be cleared quickly

    We begin with pigmentation and spots (Pico laser), moles and flat warts (MCL31), and sebaceous hyperplasia and syringomas (Agnes radiofrequency needle). For more severe acne, taking medication may be recommended. Gold PTT is an optional treatment, decided together with your medical team after consultation.

  • Sessions 5 and 7: Full skin improvement (scars and pores)

    If you have scars, we may recommend ablative fractional laser (MCL31). Please plan to keep 3 to 4 days free from outside commitments after this treatment; we will guide you on scheduling. If enlarged pores are your concern, we use Potenza (microneedle radiofrequency).

  • Sessions 9 and 11: Additional treatment after consulting the medical team

    Rather than high-intensity treatments, we refine skin texture with non-ablative fractional laser (PicoSure fractional) and peeling (Wikouno). The specific treatments may change at the discretion of the medical team.

  • Session 13: Final precision care

    We prescribe based on your scars and skin condition.

    · Rolling scars: TriFill Pro (air lifting + Juvelook)
    · Ice-pick scars: TCA dot peel
    · Firmness and texture: Dermaatome + skin botox (topical application)
    · Redness: Synergy Focus laser (vascular laser)

* For more severe cases, treatments that need recovery time, such as TriFill Pro or TCA peeling, may be started early in the course. Some treatments take two to three months before their effects appear.

Main treatments used in the package

  • Pigmentation and spots

    Pico laser: removal of spots, melasma and pigmented lesions

  • Moles, warts and sebaceous lesions

    MCL31: moles, flat warts, ablative fractional laser / Agnes radiofrequency needle: sebaceous hyperplasia, syringomas

  • Acne and sebaceous gland care

    Gold PTT: sebaceous gland reduction, improvement of inflammatory acne (optional) / Medication: regulating sebum production, reducing inflammation

  • Scars and pores

    Ablative fractional laser (MCL31): deep scars, skin regeneration / Potenza (microneedle radiofrequency): pores, skin texture, sebaceous gland reduction / TriFill Pro: rolling scars / TCA dot peel: ice-pick scars

  • Texture and tone

    Non-ablative fractional laser (PicoSure fractional): texture, tone-up / Wikouno peel: exfoliation, refining texture / Dermaatome + skin botox: firmness, texture

  • Redness and blood vessels

    Synergy Focus laser: vascular lesions, flushing, redness

After the 14 sessions

Throughout the course, we check your skin changes together every week and talk through the direction of improvement, building the story of your treatment. Even after the final care session in week 14, we continue to check your skin and discuss any care you may need.

Frequently asked questions

  • How long does the acne package take?

    It is a three-month course with one session a week, 14 sessions in total.

  • How do treatments and care sessions alternate?

    Treatment and care sessions alternate. Odd-numbered sessions are treatments and even-numbered sessions are care, with seven of each.

  • How long is the consultation?

    Usually about 20 to 30 minutes. On your first visit, we take photos with Mark View and talk with the director first.

  • Do the treatments hurt a lot?

    Some procedures require anesthesia and others do not. How much discomfort you feel differs from person to person, so we allow enough time for anesthesia.

  • Can I go about my day after treatment?

    Recovery time depends on the treatment. After ablative fractional laser, you may need to keep 3 to 4 days free from outside commitments, so please plan ahead. We will explain detailed aftercare during your consultation.

  • When will I see results?

    Some treatments take two to three months before their effects appear. Results vary from person to person.

  • If my acne is severe, will I also be prescribed medication?

    Taking medication may be recommended. Medication is prescribed after consultation with a doctor. Gold PTT is optional and is decided together with the medical team after consultation.

  • Will the director personally perform my treatments?

    Yes. The director handles everything from consultation and treatment to final care.

  • How can I find out the price?

    Pricing is not published. The director will explain it in person during your consultation.
